# Time-series bulk RNA-seq of Chronic Lymphocytic Leukaemia (CLL)

## Dataset title
Time-series bulk RNA-seq analysis of Chronic Lymphocytic Leukaemia (CLL) autologous
and monoculture in vitro models

## Authors
Hugo Chenel, Julie Bordenave, Malvina Marku, Vera Pancaldi  
Inserm / Toulouse Cancer Research Center (CRCT)

## Year
2025

## Overview
This dataset contains bulk RNA-seq profiles generated from primary chronic
lymphocytic leukaemia (CLL) samples cultured in vitro under two experimental
conditions:

1. Autologous cultures (AUTO): whole peripheral blood mononuclear cells (PBMCs).
2. Monoculture (B): purified CLL B cells obtained by negative selection.

The goal of the study is to characterize transcriptional dynamics and survival-
associated pathways during in vitro CLL progression over a 14-day time course.

## Experimental design
- Organism: Homo sapiens
- Patients: 3 (DAR, SAS, PRG)
- Time points: Day 1, Day 4, Day 8, Day 11, Day 14
- Conditions: Autologous PBMCs and purified B-CLL cells
- Replicates: 2 biological replicates per condition, patient and time point
- Total RNA-seq libraries: 60

Note: Autologous PBMC cultures and purified B-CLL monocultures were derived from the same patients, enabling paired, within-patient comparisons across culture conditions and time points.

## Sequencing
- Platform: Illumina NovaSeq 6000
- Flow cell: S1
- Layout: Paired-end
- Read length: 2 × 75 bp
- Library preparation: Illumina Stranded mRNA Prep with IDT UD indexes

## Repository structure
- `Data/Raw_data/`  
  Raw gene-level RNA-seq quantification outputs generated by RSEM, reported as
  expected counts. These files contain both Ensembl gene identifiers (ENS) and
  corresponding HGNC gene symbols, and represent per-sample quantification
  prior to normalization or downstream processing.
  
  These files correspond to 'GSE313098_Gene_count_matrix_ENS_HGCN_auto.txt.gz' and 'GSE313098_Gene_count_matrix_ENS_HGCN_B.txt.gz' in GEO supplementary files.

- `Data/Processed_data/`  
  Processed, analysis-ready gene expression matrices derived from RSEM expected
  counts and normalized to Transcripts Per Million (TPM). These matrices are
  merged across samples, reported using HGNC gene symbols or ENS identifiers only, and are intended
  for downstream comparative and time-series analyses.

- `Data/Metadata/`  
  Structured sample metadata and sample informations.

- `Data/Docs/`  
  Human-readable documentation describing the dataset, metadata, and file
  organization.
